For [Bright Signal](https://brightsignal.fm), we recently built a guest pipeline and the infrastructure for our newsletter with a few prompts. I can now ask for a dashboard or a Kanban board that fits how I work, then change it just by asking. That ease is starting to make me wonder how much I need the interface at all.

I still have to keep these tools up to date. A custom interface doesn't make me any more likely to open another app just to change a status or copy over some notes.

Could we organise more of our work around shared data and clear instructions for our agents? We'd tell the agent what changed or point it to a conversation it can access, then ask it to help plan what happens next. It would need a reliable way to work with the data and follow the rules of the process, including which decisions need us. Those rules could be quite involved without requiring us to work through a fixed set of screens.

For a team, we'd need straightforward sign-in and a way to grant or revoke access for colleagues and their agents. That setup doesn't yet feel as easy as asking for a dashboard.

I'd still want to look at the data. Sometimes browsing helps me figure out what to ask. But I might want a table today and a board tomorrow, both drawn from the same records. An agent could generate whichever view helped at that moment.

I wonder how often I ask for a dashboard simply because I'm used to working through one. If the data is there and my agent can work with it, perhaps I can leave the choice of interface until I have something I want to see.
