So... a two-ish months hiatus later, I'm back to podcasting!

After waving dataroots goodbye — and thus saying farewell to **dataroots' DataTopics Unplugged** podcast — me and Murilo are now back with **The Monkey Patching Podcast**! 🐒🖖

It's a fully independent podcast about everything new in **Data & AI**.

Yesterday, I recorded the **very first episode** together with [**Murilo Kuniyoshi Suzart Cunha**](https://be.linkedin.com/in/murilo-cunha). While we still need to find our bearings a little, there are lots of plans ahead. You can expect episodes more or less **weekly**, full of banter about data and — above all — just having fun.

🎧 All the info is available at [monkeypatching.io](https://monkeypatching.io).

Follow the podcast wherever you listen/watch, and leave a review if you like it!

Talk to you soon ❤️
