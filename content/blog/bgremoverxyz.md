Apparently I live the type of live that has me removing backgrounds from pictures every other month or so.

Is seems I am also too stingy to pay for a subscription for any of these so called "test me for free but if you actually wanna use me for anything relevant you have to pay" services.

So I created [bgremover.xyz](https://bgremover.xyz), an actually free image background remover. Taking credit for this is a bit much though, it's basically just a wrapper around [img.ly](https://img.ly/) their amazing [background-removal-js](https://github.com/imgly/background-removal-js) package.

A cool tidbit is that it works completely client-side in your browser (it leverages ONNX runtime).

It seems to work very well on actual pictures, it works so-so on digital art.

Have fun removing backgrounds, because who needs backgrounds anyway?

And if you need more features? Make sure to check out [img.ly](https://img.ly/)!
