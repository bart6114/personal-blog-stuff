I made a small thing 🏃👋

If you're into running and training methods, you might have heard about Norwegian Singles. Something that was popularized by Sirpoc in this [Letsrun thread](https://www.letsrun.com/forum/flat_read.php?thread=12130781).

Needless to say, that thread is a looong read. But, you can now check out a small summary project I've tinkered with over at [norwegiansingles.run](https://norwegiansingles.run/).

If you find yourself in that middle ground where you dedicate quite a bit of time (too much?) to running each week—more than a casual jogger but not quite at professional athlete level—consider exploring the Norwegian Singles method. It might just be the training approach you've been looking for. And who knows, it could even improve your dating prospects in Norway.

Oh and the [Quarto](https://quarto.org/) publishing system is really cool if you ever want to play with it.
