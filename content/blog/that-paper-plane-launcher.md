My kids and I have been tinkering with a [3D-printed paper-plane launcher](https://github.com/bart6114/that-paper-plane-launcher). It runs on elastic bands: pull back the slider, load a plane and squeeze the trigger.

It started with the [Ultimate Plane Launcher](https://makerworld.com/nl/models/14305-ultimate-plane-launcher) from MakerWorld, which I printed with no particular plans to change the design. The trouble was that it kept breaking. So I started reinforcing the parts to make them sturdier, and somewhere along the way it became a project my kids and I were working on together.

![The 3D-printed paper-plane launcher on a wooden table, with a black body, blue trigger and fasteners, and red and yellow parts holding the elastic bands.](/media/that-paper-plane-launcher/paper-plane-launcher.jpeg)

That has meant going back and forth on the slider, joints and catch. An extra guide underneath the slider, for example, ended up adding friction and making the launch worse, so we took it back off again.

I used to play around in Fusion 360 and Tinkercad, but I was average at best and changes like these took me quite a while. Astra has made this a lot more fun: I can describe what I'd like to change or give it an image with a few sketches on top, and get on with trying the next version.

If you want to print one, the [complete Bambu Studio project](https://github.com/bart6114/that-paper-plane-launcher/blob/main/print/Launcher_P1S.3mf) has all eleven parts arranged on two plates, ready for a Bambu Lab P1S with a 0.4 mm nozzle. Even the fasteners are printed, so you only need to add elastic bands.

I use PLA for everything except the slider, which I print in PETG for a bit more resistance to breaking. The saved projects use PLA profiles, so switch the slider to your PETG profile before slicing.

It's still a prototype, and the latest version needs more testing to see how well it holds up. The [printing and assembly notes](https://github.com/bart6114/that-paper-plane-launcher/blob/main/docs/printing.md) will help you get started. You'll also find individual part downloads and the sources in the repo, so you can change a part and print it again.

[Pick up the project](https://github.com/bart6114/that-paper-plane-launcher) and develop it further with your kids. ❤️
